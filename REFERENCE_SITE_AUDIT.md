## 5. Blog — the Big Gap

The reference has 18+ posts across 3 paginated pages. Titles suggest a clear content strategy:

- **Procedure deep-dives** (Carbon Peeling, NCTF Skin Booster, Carbon Laser vs CO2 comparison)
- **Condition guides** (Acne-diet connection, Dermatosis Papulosa Nigra)
- **Seasonal/skin-type guides** ("Top Skincare Tips for Oily Skin")
- **Press/media** ("Dr. Showrya TV interview")

### Why we need a blog
1. **SEO** — long-tail keywords ("best dermatologist RK Puram", "acne treatment Hyderabad", specific procedure queries) drive 60–80% of organic traffic for medical practices. Without a blog we're invisible on Google for anything other than branded search.
2. **Trust / authority** — educational content is the #1 signal that a doctor is knowledgeable rather than just a service provider.
3. **Reusable content** — posts can be shared on Instagram/WhatsApp, printed as patient handouts, used in consultation.

### Proposed starter blog topics (first 6 posts)

Modelled on reference's topic coverage but **written originally** via our `/write-blog` skill:

1. **"Understanding Acne in Adult Indian Skin"** — condition guide
2. **"What Actually Works for Melasma: An Evidence-Based Guide"** — condition guide
3. **"How to Read a Sunscreen Label in India"** — educational / seasonal
4. **"CO₂ vs HIFU vs Radiofrequency: Which Skin-Tightening is Right for You?"** — comparison (model reference's CO2 vs Carbon post)
5. **"Hair Fall in Women: When to See a Dermatologist"** — patient-education
6. **"Why Hyderabad's Water May Be Affecting Your Scalp"** — local + SEO gold

Each: 800–1200 words, structured with H2s, FAQ section, internal links to relevant services.

### Technical plan (later, not now)
- Route: `/blog` index + `/blog/:slug`
- MDX / markdown files in `content/posts/`
- Reuse the editorial typography from `/about`
- Per-post hero with category badge, reading time, date, author (always Dr. Yashasve)
- Related posts at bottom + service CTA
- Tag filtering (Skin / Hair / Body / Wellness / General) to match service categories

## 9. Icon Strategy — Free-Only

### Current state
We use **Lucide icons** (`Syringe`, `Droplet`, `Zap`, `Sun`, `Flame`, `Waves`, etc.). These are generic geometric SaaS-tool icons — they read as tech product, not medical practice. The reference uses warm, hand-illustrated line-art (face-with-laser, IV-drip-with-person, etc.) — which is what your screenshot flagged as missing from our site.

### Recommendation: migrate to **Phosphor Icons** (thin weight)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **License** | MIT — free for commercial use, no attribution required |
| **Size** | 9000+ icons, 6 weights (thin, light, regular, bold, fill, duotone) |
| **Best weight for us** | **Thin** — matches the warm editorial line-art of the reference closely |
| **React package** | `@phosphor-icons/react` (tree-shakeable) |
| **Migration effort** | 1–2 hours — `lucide-react` imports become `@phosphor-icons/react/dist/ssr` imports; icon names mostly map one-to-one |
| **Site** | phosphoricons.com |

### Phosphor candidates for each of our 15 services

| Service | Current (Lucide) | Proposed (Phosphor Thin) |
|---|---|---|
| Acne & Scars | Microscope | `Drop` or `Pulse` or `FirstAidKit` |
| Pigmentation | Sparkles | `Sun` or `Sparkle` |
| Hair Fall PRP/GFC | Droplet | `TestTube` or `Syringe` |
| Anti-Aging | Syringe | `Syringe` (Phosphor version is more illustrative) |
| Laser Hair Reduction | Zap | `Lightning` or `WaveTriangle` |
| Clinical Dermatology | Stethoscope | `Stethoscope` |
| Medifacials | ScanFace | `UserCircle` or `Sparkle` |
| Skin Surgery | ShieldCheck | `Scissors` or `Bandaids` |
| Carbon Peel | Flame | `Flame` |
| Laser Toning | Sun | `Sun` |
| Chemical Peel | FlaskConical | `Flask` |
| HIFU | Waves | `WaveSine` or `Waveform` |
| Cosmelan Peel | CircleDot | `Circle` or `Drop` |
| Vampire Facial | HeartPulse | `HeartStraight` or `Pulse` |
| Skin Boosters / Profhilo | Droplet | `Drop` |

### What Phosphor **won't** give us

The reference's "person getting lasered" / "person with IV drip" style icons are **custom illustrations, not from any icon pack**. Those are commissioned work. To fully match that style for free, we'd need to draw custom SVGs ourselves (~30 min per icon × 15 icons = 7–8 hours of design work). Phosphor Thin is the closest free off-the-shelf match available.

### Alternative free options considered and why Phosphor won

| Library | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **Iconoir** (MIT) | Smaller set, less medical coverage, stroke weight doesn't match |
| **Tabler Icons** (MIT) | Good and free, but thicker stroke — reads similar to Lucide |
| **Healthicons** (CC0) | WHO-style public-health style, too utilitarian for editorial site |
| **Heroicons** (MIT) | Not medical-focused |
| **Solar Icons** | CC-BY (attribution required) — more friction than MIT |

### Plan for icon migration (when you're ready)

1. Install: `npm install @phosphor-icons/react`
2. Create a central icon map (replace `iconMap` in `constants.tsx`) with Phosphor imports
3. Pass `weight="thin"` on each icon render
4. Keep Lucide for UI chrome (arrows, close buttons) where Phosphor's thin weight is too delicate
5. QA in the nav + service cards + footer

Estimate: **2–3 hours** end-to-end including visual QA.
